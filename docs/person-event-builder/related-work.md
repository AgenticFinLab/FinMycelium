# Related work on event reconstruction in FinMycelium

*Muhan Li*

FinMycelium reconstructs event processes from public sources. Its person-centered graphs add a question of attribution. A report may establish that an organization acted while leaving open who proposed, authorized, or carried out the action. That distinction matters when selecting documents, merging accounts, and drawing links between Actions.

## Event-process graphs and biographical timelines

Turner used public inquiries to trace how organizational conditions accumulated before a disaster. FinMycelium cites this work in its account of event developmental sequences. The lab's H²EPR-Bench gives event-process reconstruction a concrete form. Given an event specification and fixed sources, a system recovers stages, episodes, participants, actions, and outcomes, together with their relations and provenance. [Turner, *The Organizational and Interorganizational Development of Disasters*](https://www.jstor.org/stable/2391850) · [H²EPR-Bench dataset and task](https://huggingface.co/datasets/AgenticFinLab/H2EPR-Bench)

EventKG+BT builds biographical timelines by selecting relevant events and temporal relations from a knowledge graph. FinMycelium's research catalog groups a person's Events into thematic Storylines. The Person Event Builder then reconstructs one bounded Event in depth. A Stage covers a period with a distinct situation. A Phase groups a relatively stable pattern of behavior within that Stage, and each Action belongs to one Phase. Context can describe a condition shared across a Phase or a circumstance specific to one Action. [EventKG+BT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.06306) · [Person research catalog](../../skills/plan-person-research/SKILL.md) · [Person Event hierarchy](../person-event-structures/1-hierarchy.md)

The Event StoryLine Corpus studies temporal and causal links between events in news. MAVEN-ERE annotates coreference, temporal, causal, and subevent relations together. These tasks distinguish three judgments that also arise in a person graph. Two reports may describe the same Action. Two distinct Actions may have a known order. A claim that one prompted the other requires evidence for the connection itself. When dates overlap or the order is unresolved, the timeline can show Actions on separate rows without asserting that they happened simultaneously. [Event StoryLine Corpus](https://aclanthology.org/W17-2711/) · [MAVEN-ERE](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.60/) · [Action graph](../person-event-structures/2-action-graph.md) · [Timeline layout](../person-event-visualization/1-action-layout.md)

## Public search and passage retrieval

STORM uses questions from different perspectives to discover sources. IRCoT shows how an intermediate finding can guide the next retrieval in a multi-step question. FinMycelium's public Search likewise works from a bounded research question. It can use Baidu, Bocha, and Exa to discover documents, read the source bodies, and follow a lead. For example, a public announcement can name the participant whose later interview explains how a decision was reached. The handoff preserves selected source text for the Builder. [STORM](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.347/) · [IRCoT](https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.557/) · [Public Search workflow](../workflows/search-enhancement.md)

**BM25 for passage ranking.** BM25 is a lexical retrieval method. In public Search, it ranks passages within one retrieved document. Query terms that occur in fewer passages carry more weight, repeated occurrences within a passage have diminishing returns, and scoring accounts for passage length. A distinctive counterparty name can therefore help locate the relevant paragraph in a long annual report. The score guides reading and text selection. Deciding what the paragraph establishes requires examining the text. [Robertson and Zaragoza, *BM25 and Beyond*](https://doi.org/10.1561/1500000019) · [Passage ranking and allocation](../../finmy/search/budget.py)

LongRAG studies how to retain both a document's broader context and the factual detail in an individual passage. RECOMP allows a retrieval step to supply no additional text when the results add no information. Both ideas matter at handoff. In an interview, the preceding question can identify whose decision the speaker is explaining. FinMycelium preserves selected original text, records its source and location, and can include the adjacent exchange so that the quotation remains interpretable. [LongRAG](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.1259/) · [RECOMP](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/bda88ed2892f5e61c9a9bf215c566913-Abstract-Conference.html) · [Search-to-Builder handoff findings](../experiments/search-evidence-handoff.md)

## Event builders and use of context

Retrieval-augmented generation conditions a model's output on retrieved passages. In FinMycelium, public Search finds external material, while local retrieval during construction draws on the material already handed to the Builder. The original `AgentEventBuilder` constructs a Stage and Episode outline and assigns episode details to specialized roles. `SparseRagBuilder` varies the amount of detail by task, supports local retrieval, and reuses role results. The Person Event Builder has its own construction sequence. It extracts facts by source, merges accounts of the same Action, organizes Stages and Phases, and reviews action relations and Context together. Its roles can reopen a saved record or an exact passage in the frozen input. [Retrieval-Augmented Generation](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/hash/6b493230205f780e1bc26945df7481e5-Abstract.html) · [Sparse event reconstruction](../workflows/sparse-rag-build.md) · [Person Event construction](3-construction-flow.md)

*Lost in the Middle* found that the position of a relevant passage affects how well models use it. Anthropic's agent-memory study found that an organized index helped with some cross-document judgments, with less benefit when a single document answered the question. Multi-agent scaling research also finds that coordination depends on how independently subtasks can be solved. These results motivate giving each Builder role the records and quotations it needs. Merging benefits from seeing competing accounts side by side. Relation review benefits from seeing both Actions alongside the wording that connects them, even when it appears in a third document. [Lost in the Middle](https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.9/) · [The components of agent memory](https://www.anthropic.com/research/components-of-agent-memory) · [Towards a Science of Scaling Agent Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08296) · [FinMycelium's joint relation and Context review](../experiments/person-relation-joint-vs-split.md)

## Source attribution, evidence, and gaps

FAMuS distinguishes verifying that a source covers the target event from extracting the participants and roles it supports. ALCE evaluates both the correctness of citations and how fully they support a generated answer. In a person graph, each Evidence record identifies a quotation from one Source. An Action, a Context statement, or a relation can draw on several quotations from one or more Sources. A foundation's announcement may document a person's public pledge. A later payment by the foundation establishes a different fact. Attributing a decision about that payment to the person requires its own support. [FAMuS](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.457/) · [ALCE](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/) · [Evidence and provenance](../person-event-structures/4-evidence.md)

*Sufficient Context* distinguishes missing information from a failure to use the available evidence. RAGChecker diagnoses retrieval and generation separately. In event reconstruction, an omitted Action can be traced through three places. Did Search find the source? Did the relevant passage enter the handoff? Did the Builder use it in the graph? HALT studies how support for specific claims can guide the decision to stop searching. A useful follow-up question identifies an unresolved actor, date, or connection whose answer could change the graph. Where the available evidence still leaves the matter open, the graph can retain the gap. [Sufficient Context](https://research.google/pubs/sufficient-context-a-new-lens-on-retrieval-augmented-generation-systems/) · [RAGChecker](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/27245589131d17368cccdfa990cbf16e-Abstract.html) · [HALT](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.343/)
